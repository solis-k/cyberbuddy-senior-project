Topic: Safe Browsing

What is safe browsing:
Safe browsing means using the internet in a way that protects your information and device.

Tips:
- Only visit trusted websites
- Look for "https://" in the URL
- Avoid clicking pop-ups and ads
- Do not download files from unknown sites

Be careful:
- Fake websites can look real
- Free downloads can contain malware

Remember:
If a website looks suspicious, leave it immediately.
