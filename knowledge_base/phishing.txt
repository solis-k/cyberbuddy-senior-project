Topic: Phishing

What it is:
Phishing is when scammers try to trick you into giving personal information like passwords or account details.

Common signs:
- Urgent messages like "Act now!" or "Your account will be locked"
- Emails from strange or misspelled addresses
- Links that look suspicious or unfamiliar
- Requests for passwords or personal information

How to stay safe:
- Do not click links from unknown messages
- Check the sender’s email carefully
- Hover over links before clicking
- Ask a trusted adult or teacher if unsure

Remember:
If something feels off or too urgent, it might be a scam.
