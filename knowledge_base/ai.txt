Topic: Artificial Intelligence (AI) Safety

What is AI:
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is technology that can think, learn, and respond like a human. Examples include chatbots, voice assistants, and recommendation systems.

How AI is used:
- Chatbots (like CyberBuddy)
- Voice assistants (Siri, Alexa)
- Social media feeds
- Video and music recommendations

Risks of AI:
- AI can give incorrect or misleading information
- Deepfakes can create fake images or videos
- AI may be used in scams or phishing messages

How to stay safe:
- Do not trust everything AI says without checking
- Verify important information with trusted sources
- Be cautious of AI-generated messages or videos
- Never share personal information with unknown AI tools

Remember:
AI is helpful, but you should always think critically and stay cautious.
