Topic: Social Media Safety

Stay safe online:
- Keep your account private
- Only accept friend requests from people you know
- Be careful what you post

Never share:
- Your home address
- Your school name
- Your phone number
- Passwords

Watch out for:
- Strangers messaging you
- Links sent by unknown users
- People asking for personal info

Remember:
Once something is posted online, it can be hard to remove.
