Topic: Data Privacy

What is personal information:
- Name, address, phone number
- School name
- Passwords and account info

Why it matters:
Sharing personal information can put you at risk of scams or identity theft.

How to stay safe:
- Only share info on trusted websites
- Check app permissions before installing
- Do not overshare online

Tips:
- Turn off location sharing when not needed
- Review privacy settings on apps

Remember:
Protecting your information helps keep you safe online.
